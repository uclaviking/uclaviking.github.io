Timeline of the Saga Age

  870 Immigration and settlement of Iceland begins
  885 Harald Fairhair becomes king of Norway
  900

Unnr (or Aud) the Deep-Minded, one of the first settlers of Iceland, dies.

  930 Althing is established
  mid-960's Iceland is divided into quarters for administrative and legal purposes. Four quarter courts are established.
  c. 985 The settlement of Greenland
  999-1000 Iceland is converted to Christianity
  1005 The Fifth Court (Court of appeals) is established
  1015-30 Reign of St. Olaf of Norway
